Questions

Is OnTrack free to start?

Yes. Create a profile and you get an access code you can use to sign back in.

Will it work on my phone?

OnTrack is installable on phones and works offline after your first visit.

Where is my data kept?

On your device, and synced to your connected account when cloud sync is set up.
